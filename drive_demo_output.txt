Tools exposed by cityflo-lateness:
  - worst_routes: The headline answer: the worst `n` chronically-late routes (ranked), plus any routes
  - rank_table: One verdict per route for every route, worst first (chronic routes ranked, then event
  - route_report: Everything needed to defend (or attack) one route: median/mean/p90, peak-window median,
  - data_quality: What the cleaning did and why -- so the numbers survive someone poking at the raw file.
  - ops_context: Operational events from the week, as neutral categories only (weather, crowd, driver-ops).
